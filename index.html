Features

🚀 Launch Anything

Play any Minecraft version with Fabric, Forge, Quilt or NeoForge — one-click launch from the launcher.

📴 Works Offline

Install mods from your local .jar files and launch with cached mods even when you have no internet connection.

📦 Drag & Drop Mods

Just drop .jar files into the mod list or a profile — mods are installed and deployed automatically.

🎨 Client Options

Install client mods once and use them across every version, or auto-install them for all launched versions.

📁 Profiles

Create profiles with their own mod loader, Minecraft version, mods, datapacks and resource packs — including per-version packs.

🖼️ Resource Packs

Add resource packs from Modrinth/CurseForge, your disk, or drag & drop — always applied to the right version.

🎭 Skins & Capes

Upload skins, edit pixels, preview your skin in 3D, and use custom capes.

🌐 Servers

Manage your servers and expose them to friends with free playit.gg tunnels.

🎨 Themes

Switch between dark and light theme, pick your accent color and adjust the corner radius.

📢 Built-in Updates

Check for updates and download the new version right inside the launcher with a live progress screen.

🛠️ Crash Reports

Report bugs straight to GitHub from the settings, optionally attaching the latest logs.
